# Pitch notes: presenting the FlatProof homepage

Eight talking points for walking FlatProof through the demo: what's different from vpconstro.com, and why it should turn more visitors into bookings.

## The three-minute demo

1. Open https://flatproof.vercel.app on a laptop. Move the mouse over the room: the thermal camera locks onto damp, a mis-wired socket, a hollow tile and the window sill, and the panel counts "Found 4 of 4".
2. Click marker 1 (damp behind the skirting), then "See the sample report" in the panel: the page drops to the report, assembled and ready to hand to a builder.
3. Scroll back up through the checklist tape, the carpet-area audit (type a rate per sq ft into the calculator) and the window-sill case.
4. Open the same link on a phone: the camera tours the room on its own, and every button opens WhatsApp with the right message already typed.

## Eight points

1. **It opens with their work, not a template.** Their current site opens like any AI-generated page. This one opens on a new flat on handover day and lets the buyer play inspector: the thermal camera finds the four defects their own checklist and site visits turn up. The visitor understands the service before reading a word.

2. **It sells the report, because the report is the product.** Their sample report (FP-PUNE-2026-088) is rebuilt as a real page: a severity tag, a thumbnail and the recommended fix for each snag, plus the carpet-area audit. A buyer sees exactly what they will hand the builder, which is what they are paying for.

3. **Method beats a points race.** Pune rivals advertise 200 or 400 points. Here the 125 checks are a measuring tape split by trade, and each named check says how it is tested: polarity test, tap test, thermal camera, laser. It reads as engineering, not marketing, and it is all true.

4. **The carpet-area audit is shown in rupees.** The sample shows 31.60 sq ft short; the buyer types their own rate per sq ft and sees what that shortfall cost them. No prices are invented, and the case for booking makes itself.

5. **Real evidence, marked up like an engineer's drawing.** The window-sill photo from their Instagram carries revision clouds on its three faults. Real defects from a real visit build more trust than stock photos and badges.

6. **Every service books on WhatsApp in one tap, with the right message.** Their own prefilled messages are kept for each service, plus call, email and an Instagram DM. A floating WhatsApp button follows the reader between the hero and the booking section.

7. **Fast, accessible and honest.** On Lighthouse's mobile test (a throttled mid-range phone) the live site scored 100 for performance in four of five runs, and 100 for accessibility and best practices in every run; the main text appears in about 1.3 seconds and nothing jumps as it loads. It works with a keyboard and a screen reader and respects reduced motion. Every business claim is verified or listed for them to confirm (`CLIENT_QUESTIONS.md`); there are no made-up reviews, ratings, prices or certifications.

8. **Easy to grow.** All text lives in one content file. Seven photos dropped into a folder and one command replace the placeholders and rebuild the thermal views. The VP Constructions lineage and the inspect-then-fix story (waterproofing with a warranty) are already in place for the full site.

## What to ask for in the meeting

The 30 questions in `CLIENT_QUESTIONS.md`. The quick wins: real reviews, the engineers' names and photos, prices or price ranges, warranty terms, and approval of the white-and-teal logo on dark backgrounds.
